AI Evals in Production. Co-sponsor stacks.

A hands-on workshop tour on evaluating AI features in production. It builds on the product analytics we discussed, with LLM traces, evals, cost per task and user behavior. Each co-sponsor owns one layer of what attendees build. Here is a recap of our Oct 22 call.

What we heard

  • Engineers only. The room must be technical, not product managers.
  • Go deeper than dashboards. Anchor on traces, cost per task and evals.
  • Make it multi-tool. Co-sponsors split the cost; no Mixpanel or Amplitude.
